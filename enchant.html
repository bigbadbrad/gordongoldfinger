Realm listing prices and realm market value are the later download. Realm historical, region market value, and region sale fields are the earlier downloads. A price gap across these clocks is not a measured crafting margin.

Rates and expected counts are copied from DATA.Forever in the installed TradeSkillMaster Disenchant.lua. Quality 2 is the green row. Expected value multiplies the file's amountOfMats by each mat's Normal-Alliance min buyout. A missing buyout leaves the expected value missing. A row is green only when that value is more than 25% above a green item's own min buyout.
